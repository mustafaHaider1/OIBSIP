# Task 4 — Infographic Design

## Oasis Infobyte Graphics Design Internship

### Project Title
Cyber Safety in India: 5 Smart Habits to Stay Safe Online

### Objective
To create a clear and visually engaging infographic that presents cyber-safety information through a structured layout, data visualisation, icons, and actionable safety tips.

### Tools Used
- Canva

### Infographic Specifications
- Dimensions: 800 × 2000 px
- Format: Tall portrait infographic
- Export: High-resolution PNG

### Information Sections
1. Use strong and unique passwords
2. Enable two-factor authentication
3. Avoid unknown links, attachments, and QR codes
4. Never share OTP, PIN, CVV, or passwords
5. Update devices and report cyber fraud quickly

### Visual Elements Used
- Cybersecurity icons and illustrations
- Bar chart showing cybersecurity incident growth
- Progress bars/checklist section
- Section numbers and visual dividers

### Design Features
- Clear title at the top
- Five distinct informational sections
- Top-to-bottom visual flow
- Consistent navy, cyan, orange, and light-background colour palette
- Statistics and information sources cited at the bottom
- High-resolution PNG export

- Press Information Bureau, Government of India
- National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal: https://cybercrime.gov.in
- Indian Cyber Crime Helpline: 1930
